📐 Suite Architecture & In-Game Proportions
💡
Interactive Preview Note: This web showcase is an illustrative example. In World of Warcraft, UI scale, icon sizing, and visual proportions vary depending on your monitor resolution and graphics settings, and remain 100% customizable at any time via the /wa options panel.
1. Zero-Overlap Vertical Stacking

Core elements (Stats, Mana Bar, Utility Row) remain anchored. Alerts, Procs, and the Castbar expand smoothly upwards during combat without obscuring any active timers.

2. BiS Endgame Trinkets (DFO & CTS)

Dedicated tracking for Dislodged Foreign Object and Charred Twilight Scale, complete with accurate 45s Internal Cooldown (ICD) clock swipes.

3. Dynamic Procs Group (Auto-Centering)

Responsive cluster hosting Tier 10, Hot Streak, Clearcasting, Living Bomb, Ignite, Scorch, and Molten Fury (≤35% HP). Inactive procs collapse cleanly.

4. Decoupled 2-Segment Hot Streak

Left segment tracks your persistent 1st direct crit (never expires out of combat). Right segment tracks the 10s Hot Streak proc with golden Pixel Glow and instant Pyroblast alert.

5. 4-Row Live Stats Engine

Real-time calculation of Spell Power, Crit, Haste, and Hit, dynamically updating as armors, trinket procs, raid buffs, and tier bonuses activate.

6. WotLK Tier Set Progression

Supports T7 (Mana Surge on gem), T8 (Praxis SP in utility row), and T10 (Pushing the Limit haste + Quad Core damage) with one-click presets.

7. Multi-Target Living Bomb Tracker

Dedicated right-side vertical column tracking up to 5 active Living Bombs sorted by shortest expiration time, with ≤3s red alert countdowns.
